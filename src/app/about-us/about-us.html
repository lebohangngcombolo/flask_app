About i-STOKVEL

We're revolutionizing the way communities save and grow together

Our Mission

To provide secure, transparent, and efficient digital stokvel management solutions that empower communities to achieve their financial goals together.

We believe in the power of collective savings and are committed to making traditional stokvel practices more accessible, secure, and rewarding for everyone.

Our Vision

To become the leading digital platform for community-based financial services across Africa, fostering financial inclusion and economic empowerment.

We envision a future where every community can easily manage their collective finances with confidence, transparency, and security.
